# TalkBoard User Guide

TalkBoard is a picture communication board (AAC, augmentative and alternative communication). Your child taps a picture and the tablet says the word out loud. Pictures can also show a short ASL sign video.

This guide is for parents, caregivers, and anyone else who sets up or helps with the board.

## 1. Getting started on the tablet

**Install the app**
1. Open **https://talkboard-ten.vercel.app** in **Chrome** on the tablet.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app** (on some tablets, **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open TalkBoard from its home-screen icon. It runs full-screen and works without internet. The only exception is YouTube sign videos.

**Set up the voice**
1. Open the tablet's **Settings** and search for **Text-to-speech**.
2. Choose **Speech Services by Google** as the engine.
3. Under its settings, download the **English** voice data if it isn't already installed.
4. In TalkBoard, open Settings → **Voice** to pick a specific voice and speed.

**Lock the tablet to TalkBoard (recommended)**
App pinning keeps your child from swiping out of the app.
1. Tablet **Settings** → search for **pin** → turn on **App pinning** (sometimes called "Pin windows"). Turn on "Ask for PIN before unpinning."
2. Open TalkBoard, open **Recent apps** (the square button or swipe up and hold), tap the TalkBoard icon at the top of its card, and choose **Pin**.
3. To unpin, hold **Back** and **Recent apps** together (or follow the on-screen hint), then enter your PIN.

**Using the Android app with Samsung Kids**
The TalkBoard Android app (install from https://github.com/bparker2311/talkboard/releases/latest/download/TalkBoard.apk) can be added to Samsung Kids under **Parental controls → Apps**.
- Samsung Kids blocks the file picker, so **adding photos, uploading videos or audio, and saving or restoring backups only work outside Samsung Kids**. Exit Samsung Kids, open TalkBoard from the normal app drawer, make your changes, then go back into Samsung Kids. It's the same app and the same board, so the changes carry over.
- Typing words, emoji, YouTube links, colors, rearranging and settings all work inside Samsung Kids too (in parent mode).

## 2. The screen at a glance

From top to bottom:

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sentence bar** | Words your child taps collect here, like "I want more crackers." |
| 🔊 | Speaks the whole sentence. |
| ⌫ | Removes the last word. |
| ✕ | Clears the sentence. |
| 🔒 | Hold for 2 seconds to enter **parent mode**. |
| **Core words strip** | Always-visible words used all day: *I, want, more, stop, go, help, all done, yes, no, like*. |
| **Category tabs** | People, Food, Feelings, Play, Places, Numbers, Letters, Signs, and any you add. |
| **Picture grid** | The pictures for the selected category. |
| **◀ ▶ page buttons** | Appear when a category has more than one page. |

A small 🤟 badge on a picture means it has a sign video.

## 3. How your child uses it

- **Tap a picture** to hear the word. It's added to the sentence bar.
- **Tap 🔊** to hear the whole sentence.
- **Tap 🤟** on a picture to watch the ASL sign. Tap **Again** to replay it or **Done** to close it.
- **Tap a tab** to switch categories, and **◀ ▶** to switch pages.

Depending on the settings (see [Touch](#touch)), she may need to hold her finger on a picture for a moment. A blue fill rises up the picture, and it speaks when the fill reaches the top.

## 4. Parent mode

All editing happens in parent mode, so your child can't change the board by accident.

- **To enter:** press and **hold 🔒 for 2 seconds**. A ring fills around the lock, which then turns orange. A yellow banner appears.
- **To exit:** tap 🔒 once.

In parent mode:
- Every picture shows a ✎. **Tap a picture** to edit it.
- Empty spots show **+**. **Tap +** to add a picture there.
- **⚙️ Settings & categories** in the yellow banner opens Settings.
- Hidden categories appear dimmed with 🙈.

## 5. Adding and editing pictures

Tap a picture or a **+** in parent mode to open the editor.

**Word and sound**
- **Word**: the label shown on the picture, like *Mom*.
- **Says**: what the tablet actually says, if different from the label. Leave it blank to use the word. Tap 🔊 next to it to hear it before saving. Use this to fix words the voice mispronounces. For example, if a letter sounds wrong, type it spelled out the way it should sound.
- **Emoji**: a quick placeholder picture. Tap the box, then tap the 😊 key on the keyboard and use its search bar (type "pizza", "dog", and so on).

**Picture**
- **📷 Choose / take photo**: use a photo from the gallery or take one with the camera. A **Frame the photo** box opens. Drag the photo to move it, and use the slider or pinch to zoom, until the face or object fills the square. Then tap **Use photo**.
- **✂️ Adjust crop**: re-frame a photo later without picking it again. This works for photos added after this feature was added; for older ones, choose the photo again.
- **Remove photo**: goes back to the emoji (or the plain word if there's no emoji).
- Real photos (her cup, her grandma, her favorite toy) are usually easier for a young child to recognize than emoji.

**Color**
Pick a background color. **Soft** colors are for grouping words. **Bright** colors (red, blue, black, and so on) are for things like a Colors category: leave the emoji empty, and the word shows large in the middle of the color. The 🌈 swatch picks any other color. Text switches to white automatically on dark colors.

For grouping words by soft color: A common convention (the Fitzgerald Key) groups words by color:

| Color | Used for |
|---|---|
| Yellow | People |
| Green | Actions (want, go, eat) |
| Orange | Things, food, places |
| Blue | Feelings and describing words |
| Pink | Social words (please, thank you, yes, no) |

**Voice**
- **🎙️ Record voice**: record your own voice saying the word. Tap again to stop. That recording plays instead of the tablet voice.
- **Upload audio**: use an audio file instead.
- **▶ Play / Remove**: listen to or delete the recording.

**ASL sign video:** see [section 7](#7-asl-sign-videos).

**Saving**
- **Save** keeps your changes, **Cancel** throws them away, and **Delete** removes the picture. You'll be asked to confirm a delete.

## 6. Rearranging pictures

In parent mode:

- **Drag to swap:** press and hold a picture for about a third of a second, then drag it onto another picture to swap them, or onto an empty spot to move it.
- **Move to another category:** drag a picture onto a **category tab**. It goes into the first empty spot in that category.
- **Move to another page:** while dragging, hold the picture over **◀** or **▶** for about half a second and the page flips.
- **Fine moves:** in the editor, **◀ Move earlier / Move later ▶** shifts a picture one spot at a time.

> **Keep pictures in the same place once she knows them.** Kids learn where words are by muscle memory, the way adults type without looking. Add new words in empty spots instead of reshuffling.

## 7. ASL sign videos

Any picture can show a sign video when the 🤟 badge is tapped.

**Using a YouTube video**
1. In the editor, tap **🔎 Search YouTube** (searches "ASL sign *word*") or **📘 Lifeprint dictionary** (opens that word's page at Lifeprint.com, a free ASL dictionary).
2. Find a good clip and **watch it yourself first**.
3. Tap **Share → Copy link**. If the sign starts partway into the video, turn on **Start at** in the share menu first.
4. Go back to TalkBoard and paste the link into **YouTube link**.
5. Optionally fill in **Start / end** (seconds like `12`, or minutes:seconds like `0:12`) so only the sign plays.
6. Tap **Preview sign** to check it, then **Save**.

YouTube videos need internet, and a video can disappear if its owner removes it. When the tablet is offline, the 🤟 badge on YouTube-only words turns gray and does nothing, so she doesn't hit a blank screen. Words with your own videos keep working. The video plays muted and without suggested videos, but YouTube's logo still appears. App pinning keeps her from leaving the app through it.

**Using your own video**
Tap **🎬 Use my own video instead** and pick a clip from the tablet. A 3 to 5 second clip of you or a family member signing works best. It works offline, has no ads, and shows a familiar face. Your own video takes priority over a YouTube link.

**Tips**
- Signs vary between sources. If a speech therapist or teacher works with her, ask which version they use so everyone signs the same way.
- Start with about 10 everyday words: *more, all done, eat, drink, milk, help, please, Mom, Dad, potty.*
- The **Signs** tab holds common phrases. You can add sign videos to pictures in any tab.

## 8. Settings

Enter parent mode, then tap **⚙️ Settings & categories**.

### Grid
| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Columns** | Pictures per row (3–8). Fewer columns means bigger pictures. |
| **Layout** | **Pages (no scrolling)**: each category fits one screen, with extra pictures on more pages. **Scrolling list**: one long scrolling grid. Pages are recommended. |
| **Rows per page** | How many rows fit on one page (pages layout only). |
| **Sentence bar** | Show the sentence bar, or hide it so each word just speaks on its own. |
| **Clear & ⌫ buttons** | Hide these if she gets stuck pressing them. |

### Touch
| Setting | What it does | Suggested start |
|---|---|---|
| **Hold to select** | How long her finger must stay on a picture before it speaks. Quick brushes and swipes do nothing. | Off. Try 0.25 sec if she triggers things by accident. |
| **Tap cooldown** | After a word speaks, other taps are ignored for this long. Stops rapid-fire tapping. | 0.5 sec |
| **Ignore palms** | A palm, or two or more fingers on the screen, triggers nothing. | On |

### Voice
| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Voice** | Which tablet voice to use. |
| **Speed** | How fast it talks. Slightly slower (0.85–0.9×) is often easier for young kids. |
| **Test voice** | Plays a sample sentence. |

### Categories
Each category has:
- **Emoji and name**: tap to edit.
- **👁️ / 🙈**: show or hide it for your child. Hidden categories still show in parent mode. Start with a few categories and add more as she's ready.
- **▲ ▼**: change the tab order.
- **✕**: delete the category and its pictures. You'll be asked to confirm.

**+ Add category** creates a new one.

## 9. Backups

**Everything you add (photos, recordings, videos, layout) is stored only on this tablet.** Uninstalling the app, clearing Chrome's data, or losing the tablet erases it.

- **⬇ Save backup file**: saves a backup to the tablet's **Downloads** folder. Copy it to Google Drive or email it to yourself.
- **⬆ Restore from backup**: loads a backup file and replaces the current board. The voice choice is the one setting that isn't copied, because voices differ between devices. Each device keeps its own.
- **Reset to starter board**: erases everything and starts over with the original pictures.

Save a backup after any big round of changes.

## 10. Tips for teaching with the board

- **Model, don't test.** The most effective thing you can do is use the board yourself while you talk to her: "Do you want **more**? **More** crackers!" (tapping *more* as you say it). Don't require her to tap before she gets something. Kids learn AAC the way they learn speech, by seeing it used around them.
- **Expect exploring.** Tapping everything at first is normal. It's how she learns what each picture says, much like a baby babbling. It usually becomes purposeful over time.
- **Start small.** Hide most categories at first. Show the core words plus People and Food, and add more as she gets comfortable.
- **Keep it available.** The board works best when it's always within reach, not only during "practice."
- **Respond to every tap.** If she taps *go*, treat it as communication: "You want to **go**? Let's **go**!"
- **Include her team.** Share the board with her speech therapist or teacher. They may suggest vocabulary or a grid size that fits her.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **No sound** | Check the volume and that the tablet isn't on mute. Make sure a text-to-speech voice is installed (see [section 1](#1-getting-started-on-the-tablet)). Try **Test voice** in Settings. |
| **A word is pronounced wrong** | Edit the picture and type how it should sound in **Says**. Tap 🔊 to check. |
| **Taps aren't working** | Check **Hold to select**. If it's on, she has to hold her finger down. Also check **Ignore palms**. If the tablet reports normal taps as large touches, turn it off. |
| **Sign video won't play** | YouTube videos need internet. Check the link with **Preview sign**. The video may have been removed. Your own uploaded videos always work offline. |
| **Can't record voice** | Allow microphone access for the site in Chrome (lock icon in the address bar → Permissions), or use **Upload audio**. |
| **App looks out of date** | Close TalkBoard completely and reopen it while online. |
| **Can't get into parent mode** | Hold the 🔒 for a full 2 seconds without moving your finger. |
| **Everything disappeared** | The tablet's browser data was probably cleared. Restore your latest backup (Settings → **Restore from backup**). |
